Methodology

What am I looking at?

The return estimates come from a regression-based forecasting model applied to historical index and equity-market data, expressed as annualised total return over holding periods of 2–10 years. "Realised" figures are the return a market actually delivered once that many years had passed; "forecast" figures are the model's estimate made at that point in time. Comparing the two over history is a way of judging how much weight the current, still-open forecasts deserve — it is not a guarantee that the pattern will repeat.
