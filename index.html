Methodology

What am I looking at?

Chart 1 shows forecasted annualised total return (price change + dividend growth) at holding periods of 2–10 years per stock market. Chart 2 shows historical and forecasted price and dividend levels of stock markets. Underlying data comes from public market sources. Forecasted returns and price and dividend levels are produced by a regression-based forecasting model. Forecasts are empirically and in-sample most accurate for 10-year periods and least accurate for 2-year periods. Both data and forecasts are updated at the start of each month.

This site presents statistical model output for general information purposes only. It is not investment, legal or tax advice, and nothing here should be relied upon as a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any financial instrument. Investing involves risk, including loss of capital.
